The gap

Millions of Muslims read the Quran daily — reciting words whose meaning they've never actually learned. Translation tells you what a verse means. It doesn't teach you the language itself. Over time, the gap between what you say and what you understand doesn't close — it just becomes familiar.

How it works

Learn one root, understand a family of words

Every word in the Quran comes from a three-letter root. Learn أ-م-ن once, and إِيمَان (faith), مُؤْمِن (believer), أَمَانَة (trust), and أَمْن (security) stop being four words to memorize — they become one idea, seen from four angles.
