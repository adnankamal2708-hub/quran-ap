How it works

From your first word to reading with meaning

01

Choose your tracks

Your tracks live in the Learn tab, with your main track first. Each card shows your level and how much of the Quran it has opened up for you.

02

Find your level in about five minutes

An optional placement test adapts to your answers: harder after a right one, easier after a wrong one. It ends as soon as your level is clear.

  • Your level, from Beginner to Advanced
  • What you're strong in, and what to work on
  • A starting lesson that skips what you know
03

Follow a clear path

Four levels of numbered lessons, starting with the Foundation Course: the 100 most frequent words in the Quran, which alone make up about 30% of what you read in it.

04

Learn by root

Most Quranic words grow from a three-letter root. Each root lesson shows the root, its meaning, how often it appears, and its whole family of words, then practises them. Part of Premium.

05

Read with meanings

Open any surah and turn on meanings: every word you've met shows its English under it, alongside the verse translation.
