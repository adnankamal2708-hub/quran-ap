Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Bayan ("we," "us," "our") is a Quranic Arabic vocabulary learning app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled. We've kept this short and honest — no legal filler.

What we collect

If you use Bayan without an account (Guest mode):
Your learning progress (reviews, streaks, notes, bookmarks) is stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. We never see it, and it isn't sent to us. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, this progress is lost — we recommend creating an account if you want your progress saved permanently.

If you create an account:

  • Email address and password — used to sign you in. Your password is never stored by us in readable form; it's securely hashed by Firebase Authentication, the service we use for sign-in.
  • Display name and avatar — if you set one.
  • Your learning progress — the same data as Guest mode (reviews, streaks, notes, bookmarks, lesson progress), backed up to our database so it's available across your devices. This requires an active Premium subscription.

If you subscribe to Premium:
We use Polar to process payments. When you start a checkout, we send Polar your email address and account ID so your subscription can be linked to your account. We never see or store your payment card details — those go directly from your browser to Polar, which handles payment processing on our behalf as our Merchant of Record. We only store your subscription status (active, plan type, renewal date) so the app knows what features to unlock.

Incidental technical data:
Like virtually every website, loading Bayan causes your browser to contact a small number of infrastructure services — Google Fonts (for typography) and Firebase's SDK (for authentication and data storage). These services incidentally receive your IP address and browser information as part of normal internet requests, the same as visiting any website. This happens even if you're using Guest mode. We don't use this for tracking or advertising.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or any third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not use cookies. Your session is kept using local browser storage only.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not send you marketing emails beyond what's necessary for your account (e.g. email verification, password reset).
  • We do not track your usage across other websites or apps.

Your in-app analytics stay on your device

The "Analytics" section inside Bayan (your learning trends, accuracy, retention stats) is calculated entirely on your own device from your local learning data. It only leaves your device as part of the Premium cloud-sync backup described above — it is never used by us for any other purpose.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you'd like your account and data deleted, contact us at probbplayer12@gmail.com and we'll remove it, except where we're required to retain limited billing records for legal/tax purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at probbplayer12@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Bayan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Bayan after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at probbplayer12@gmail.com.
